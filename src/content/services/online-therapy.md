Online sessions are offered alongside in-person work in Bath, for families who cannot attend in person. They take place in a secure, confidential virtual setting.

Families across the UK can work with me online, and the same approach applies as in the room: the sessions are shaped around the child or young person, and parents are involved as part of the work.
