Adolescence asks a lot of a person. Identity is forming, independence is growing and the pressure from every direction can feel enormous.

Therapy gives a young person a private, confidential space to make sense of their concerns. My approach is psychoanalytic. It helps young people gain insight into what is happening inside them, build resilience, and work towards positive change.

## What parents can expect

Parents are involved at the start, and the young person's sessions stay confidential. For the first meeting, parents of 12 to 15 year olds may attend with or without their teenager. At 16 and over, I recommend the young person comes alone, though a parent can come for support. There is more on this in [your first session](/first-session).

Sessions are available in English and Spanish.
