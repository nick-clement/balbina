Children often show us how they feel through what they do. A child who lashes out, cannot face school or struggles to settle may be telling us something that words cannot yet hold.

In sessions, each child is offered a safe, private space to explore their thoughts and emotions at their own pace. My approach is attachment focused and trauma informed, and I shape the work around the child in front of me.

## Younger children

Children under 4 come with a parent. We focus on emotional development, attachment and communication together.

## Children aged 4 to 12

The work centres on the child's inner world and on building self-awareness, so that feelings become easier to understand and to live with.

## Parents are part of the work

Understanding what a child is communicating takes the whole family. I meet with parents and carers alongside the child's sessions. You can read more about [working with parents](/services/parent-work).
