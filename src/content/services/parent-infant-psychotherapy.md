The first two years lay down the foundations for how a child feels about themselves and other people. Sometimes bonding does not come easily, and sometimes the early months are harder than anyone expected.

Parent-infant psychotherapy is for parents and babies from birth to around 2 years old. In sessions we observe and reflect on the way you and your baby connect, with the aim of supporting bonding and secure attachment.

Sessions are available in English and Spanish.
