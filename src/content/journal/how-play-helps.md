Adults often work through problems by talking. Children do it by playing. Play is how they show what they think and feel, and how they try out ideas that are too hard to say.

## What a therapist sees

In a therapy room, a child might build, draw, or act out stories with toys. The themes that come up, such as a family of animals, a storm, or a rescue, often reflect what is on their mind. The therapist pays close attention and helps the child make sense of it, at the child's own pace.

## Why it works

A child who feels understood, in a safe and steady relationship, can begin to feel differently about themselves and their world. Play gives them a way to do that without having to explain.

## What it looks like for parents

Parents are part of the process. A good therapist keeps you informed and works with you so that the changes carry over to home and school.
