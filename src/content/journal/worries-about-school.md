Some children find school hard in ways that are easy to miss. They may complain of tummy aches, cry at drop-off, or become irritable on Sunday evenings. These are often signs of worry rather than refusal.

## Start by listening

Ask open questions at a relaxed moment, such as in the car or while walking. "What is the hardest part of the day?" often gets a more honest answer than "Did you have a good day?"

## Keep mornings calm and predictable

A steady routine, a short and warm goodbye, and a clear plan for the day help more than long reassurances at the gate.

## Work with the school

Tell the class teacher what you are seeing. Many schools have a pastoral lead who can help with a gradual plan.

## When to seek more help

If your child is missing school regularly, or the worry is spreading into other parts of life, speak to your GP or a child and adolescent psychotherapist. Early support makes a difference.
