Separation is a big change for a child, whatever their age. Most children cope best when they feel safe, informed and free from taking sides.

## Tell them together, and keep it simple

If possible, both parents should explain the news together in calm, age-appropriate words. Make it clear that the decision is between the adults and that it is not the child's fault.

## Keep routines steady

Familiar mealtimes, school routines and bedtimes give children a sense of stability while everything else changes.

## Keep them out of the middle

Avoid criticising the other parent in front of your child, and never use them to pass messages. Children need to feel free to love both parents.

## Watch for changes

Sadness, anger, withdrawal and changes at school are common. If they continue or get worse, talking to a therapist can give your child a place of their own to work through what they feel.
