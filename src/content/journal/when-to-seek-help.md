All children have difficult days, weeks and phases. Knowing when to ask for help can be hard. A few questions can guide you.

## Is it lasting?

A change that continues for several weeks, rather than passing, deserves attention.

## Is it getting in the way?

Look at friendships, school, sleep, eating and family life. When a worry or behaviour is affecting several of these, extra support can help.

## Has something changed?

A big shift in mood, behaviour or confidence, or a change after a loss, a move or a difficult event, is worth taking seriously.

## Trust your sense of your child

You know them best. If something feels off, it is fine to ask for advice, and you do not need to wait for things to get worse.

## Where to start

Your GP or your child's school are good first steps. You can also book a free 15-minute call with me to talk through what you are noticing. If you believe a child is in immediate danger, call 999.
