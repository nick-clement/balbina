A new baby brings joy, and it also brings a big change for the child who was the youngest until now. Mixed feelings are normal. An older child may be excited one minute and cross or clingy the next.

## Expect some regression

Young children sometimes go back to baby behaviour, such as wanting a bottle or having toileting accidents. This is usually temporary and eases when they feel secure.

## Protect one-to-one time

Even ten minutes a day of undivided attention, with the phone away, tells your child they still matter. Let them choose the activity.

## Give them a role

Helping with small jobs, like fetching a nappy or choosing a hat, can help a child feel included without pressure.

## Name the feelings

"It is hard sharing Mummy sometimes" shows that all their feelings are allowed. If your child's distress is intense or lasts a long time, extra support can help the whole family settle.
