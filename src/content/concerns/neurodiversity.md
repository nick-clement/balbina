Neurodivergent children, including those who are autistic, have ADHD or learn differently, often face a world that is not built around them. That can bring worry, frustration, exhaustion, loneliness or a sense of not fitting in.

## Support, not fixing

Therapy is not about changing who your child is. It is about helping them understand and feel at ease with themselves, and supporting the feelings that can come with being different. Sessions are adapted to your child's pace, communication style and sensory needs.

## What it can offer

A steady relationship and a private space can help a child express feelings that are hard to put into words. Parent sessions help you understand what your child is communicating, strengthen your confidence and ease stress at home. My approach is attachment focused and trauma informed.

## Assessment and diagnosis

I do not diagnose. If you are seeking an assessment, your GP or your child's school can advise on the route. Therapy can sit alongside that process, and alongside support in school.

If you would like to talk through what might help, the first call is free.
